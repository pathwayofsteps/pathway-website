Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

  • Our apps don't collect any personal information.
  • Everything you create in an app stays on your device.
  • We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.
  • We don't sell or share data. We never receive any.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to each app that links to this page (an "App"). "We," "us," and "our" mean the developer of the App. It covers only Apps that link here.

2. Information we collect

None. The Apps don't send any information to us or to anyone else. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports.

The Apps contain no analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and they don't track you across other apps or websites.

If an App asks for access to something on your device, such as your camera, photos, or files, that access is used only on your device for the feature you asked for. Nothing is uploaded.

3. What you put into the Apps

Anything you type, draw, import, or create in an App ("Your Content") is stored only on your device, in that App's own storage. It is never sent to us. We can't see it, access it, or recover it for you.

Deleting an App removes Your Content from your device, apart from any copies in backups you control.

4. Sharing

We don't sell, rent, share, or trade information, because we don't have any.

If you choose to export or share something from an App, for example with the share button or by saving to Files, it goes only where you send it. Whatever service you send it to is governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Apple and your device

The Apps are distributed through Apple's App Store. Some things happen at the device or App Store level, outside the Apps and outside our control:

  • Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, the backup may include App data. Those backups are managed by you and Apple.
  • Downloads and payments. Apple handles App Store downloads and any payments. We never receive your payment details.
  • Diagnostics. If you've turned on Apple's "Share with App Developers" setting, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects these, they don't identify you, and you can turn the setting off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Apple's handling of data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

This web page has no cookies, analytics, or tracking. Like most websites, the service that hosts it may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

6. Children

The Apps don't collect personal information from anyone, so they don't collect it from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live) either.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You stay in control: delete Your Content inside the App, or delete the App to remove its data from your device.

8. Security

Your Content is protected by your device's own security. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. Anyone who can unlock your device may be able to see what's in the Apps.
